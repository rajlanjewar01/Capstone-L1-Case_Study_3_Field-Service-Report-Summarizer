Central plant - multiple

FSR-3011
2026-03-10 OK

A scheduled annual inspection of the central plant was carried out on 10 March 2026, with the engineer on site from 07:00 to 18:30 — a total of 11 hours 30 minutes. The inspection covered six asset types: chillers CH-01 to CH-03, boilers BLR-01 to BLR-03, cooling towers CT-01 and CT-02, air handling units AHU-01 to AHU-06, and the main control panel. On chiller CH-02, the main electrical contactor was found to have pitted contacts and was replaced; the same condition was found on chiller CH-03 and its contactor was also replaced as a precaution. Both chillers were otherwise in good condition. Boiler BLR-01 passed combustion testing with no action required. On boiler BLR-02, a temperature sensor was found to be reading approximately 4 degrees too high and was replaced; on boiler BLR-03, a second temperature sensor had failed entirely and was also replaced and verified. Cooling tower CT-01 had its basin cleaned, the water filter replaced, and the water treatment dosing system confirmed as working correctly. Cooling tower CT-02 was inspected and the fan bearings were found to be noisy — this has been logged for a follow-up visit. All six air handling unit filters were checked and found to be within their service interval; however, the door seal on AHU-03 had perished and was replaced to restore a proper seal. In the main control panel, a relay was found to be operating intermittently during testing and was replaced. All plant was left fully operational at the end of the visit. Two follow-up actions are recommended: the fan bearings on cooling tower CT-02 should be attended to within 30 days, and given that two contactors from the same equipment batch were found in a similar failed condition, a review of the contactor inspection and replacement schedule across the plant is advised.

Parts fitted: Electrical contactor for chiller CH-02 (replacement for pitted contacts), Electrical contactor for chiller CH-03 (preventive replacement, same condition found), Temperature sensor for boiler BLR-02 (replacement for inaccurate sensor), Temperature sensor for boiler BLR-03 (replacement for failed/open-circuit sensor), Cooling tower CT-01 water filter, Access panel door gasket for air handling unit AHU-03, Control panel relay (replacement for intermittent relay)

Outstanding: 1. Cooling tower CT-02 fan bearings are worn and producing abnormal noise. A follow-up visit is recommended within 30 days to inspect and replace the bearings before they deteriorate further. 2. Two contactors from the same equipment batch were found to have failed in similar condition. Northgate recommends a review of the replacement interval for contactors across the central plant to determine whether others from the same batch should be proactively checked or changed.

Time on site: 11 hours 30 minutes
